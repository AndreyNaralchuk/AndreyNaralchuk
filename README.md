## About

PLM Engineer with 20 years of experience. I do digitalization and automation of
Product Lifecycle Management systems, for large industrial companies.

I develop on the server side and on the client side: Web, XML, analytics, DevOps,
pipelines and workflows. In free time I build my own projects.

- **Now:** delivery for about 7 large companies.
- **Learning:** Web and AI.
- **Ask me about:** Teamcenter customization, ITK, Active Workspace, BMIDE, and PLM
  automation.
- **Outside work:** time in wild nature, with family and friends.
